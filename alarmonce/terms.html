Alarm delivery

Alarm delivery depends on compatible hardware and software, iOS permission, device power, system behavior, and the alarm remaining registered. A device that is powered off at the scheduled time may not deliver the alarm later. OnceAlarm shows the system registration state it can verify, but cannot guarantee that every alarm will sound.

Time zones and device changes

An alarm represents a specific instant. If the device time zone changes, the displayed local time can change while that instant stays the same. Daylight-saving transitions can also make a local wall-clock time invalid or ambiguous. Review the confirmation shown by the app, especially before travel or a clock change.
