OnceAlarm

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

OnceAlarm has no account, server, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Alarm information stays on your device and is handed only to iOS AlarmKit on that device so the system can deliver alarms.

Information stored on your device

  • Optional alarm titles you enter.
  • Alarm time, original time zone, enabled state, and lifecycle status.
  • A random installation identifier used only to distinguish this installation’s system registrations.
  • Appearance and language preferences.

This information is used only for app functionality and is not transmitted to SynoriTech.

System alarms and permission

When you save an enabled alarm, OnceAlarm asks iOS AlarmKit on the same device to register it. iOS controls permission and alarm delivery. You can review or change permission in system settings. Alarm information is not sent to a SynoriTech server.

What we do not do

No account creation, cloud sync, remote push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifier, advertising, or tracking. OnceAlarm does not collect payment information; the App Store handles the paid download.

Retention and deletion

You can delete one alarm in the app or use Settings → Delete all alarms. OnceAlarm first asks iOS to cancel registered alarms before removing the local records. Deleting the app removes its remaining local data. Because SynoriTech has no copy, it cannot restore deleted alarm information.

Sensitive alarm titles

Alarm titles can contain personal information. Avoid including sensitive details if they may appear on a visible system alarm screen. Do not send sensitive alarm titles in support email.

Website hosting

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical information when you visit it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. SynoriTech does not add analytics or advertising to this page.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. Questions can be sent to support@synoritech.com.
