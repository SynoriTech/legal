Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

ClefLark is designed to work offline. We do not operate user accounts or a backend service, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the ClefLark mobile application for iOS, iPadOS, and Android.

2. Data stored on your device

ClefLark stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Practice attempts and learning progress
  • App preferences and language settings
  • Trial status and purchase entitlement
  • Your preferred MIDI device identifier

This information is used only to provide app features. It is not sent to ClefLark or to an external server.

3. Microphone access

If you enable microphone input, ClefLark analyzes audio in real time on your device to identify played musical notes.

Audio is not recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted. Microphone access is optional and may be revoked at any time in your device’s system settings. The app remains usable with touch input when microphone access is disabled.

4. MIDI devices

If you connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard, ClefLark reads MIDI note events locally. A device identifier may be stored locally so that the app can reconnect to your preferred keyboard.

MIDI events and device information are not transmitted externally.

5. Purchases

Purchases, restorations, and refunds are processed by Apple or Google according to their respective policies.

ClefLark receives only the transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock purchased features. ClefLark does not receive or store your payment-card details.

6. Data export

When you choose Export practice data, ClefLark creates an export from information stored locally and copies it to the system clipboard.

Data leaves the app only when you initiate this action. After export, the data is governed by the privacy practices of the destination you choose.

7. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

ClefLark does not include advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services.

8. Retention and deletion

Local practice data and settings remain on your device until you clear the app’s data or remove the app.

Trial or purchase-entitlement records stored in secure device storage may remain after reinstalling the app. This helps preserve trial and purchase status.

You may revoke microphone or Bluetooth permissions through your device’s system settings.

9. Children’s privacy

ClefLark does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The app does not provide accounts, social features, targeted advertising, or user-to-user communication.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to ClefLark, applicable laws, or app-store requirements. The current effective date will always be displayed at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ClefLark developer at support@synoritech.com.
