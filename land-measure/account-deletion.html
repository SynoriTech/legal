LandMeasure

Account & Local Data

LandMeasure does not create user accounts.

There is no LandMeasure account or SynoriTech server record to delete. Your projects are stored on your device.

Delete one measurement

Delete an individual saved measurement from the LandMeasure project list. This removes the local project from the app.

Delete all local app data

Delete LandMeasure from your device to remove all locally stored projects and settings. Apple separately retains App Store download history under its own policies.
