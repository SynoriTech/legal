LandMeasure

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

LandMeasure has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Measurement projects stay on your device. Apple processes map, location, and App Store purchase information needed for the features you choose to use.

Information stored on your device

Project names, boundary coordinates, dates, calculated area and perimeter, units, preferences, and a local purchase-entitlement cache are stored on your device. SynoriTech does not receive this information.

Location and Apple Maps

Location access is optional and used only while the app is open, when you request your position or use walk mode. Apple Maps and Core Location process location and map requests under Apple’s privacy practices. LandMeasure does not create a background location history or send your projects to a SynoriTech server.

If you deny or revoke location access, you can still measure by choosing points on the map manually.

Purchases

Apple processes the one-time lifetime purchase and restorations through the App Store. LandMeasure receives only the product, transaction status, and verification information needed to unlock the feature. SynoriTech does not receive your full payment-card details.

Exports

When you choose to share a text summary, CSV file, or map snapshot, LandMeasure creates it on your device and presents the iOS share sheet. Data leaves the app only when you select a destination. The destination’s privacy practices then apply.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual saved measurements in the app. Uninstalling LandMeasure removes its locally stored projects, preferences, and entitlement cache. Apple retains App Store purchase history under its own policies so an eligible purchase can be restored.

Website hosting

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical information when you visit it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. SynoriTech does not add analytics or advertising to this page.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions can be sent to support@synoritech.com.
