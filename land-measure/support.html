Location and maps

Location is optional. If My Location or walk mode does not work, open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → LandMeasure and allow access while using the app. Map content needs a connection, while saved project details remain available offline.

Measurements

Add at least three boundary points. You can undo, move or delete a point, or clear the measurement and start again. Results are estimates and cannot replace a legal or professional survey.

Walk mode

Walk mode records boundary points only while LandMeasure is open in the foreground. Keep a safe distance from hazards, watch your surroundings, and review the points before saving.

Restore lifetime access

Open LandMeasure Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the original purchase. There is no subscription to manage.

Contacting us

Include the app version, iOS version, steps, expected result, and actual result. Do not send payment details or confidential project names or coordinates. We aim to reply within three business days.
