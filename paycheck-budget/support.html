Email Support

Why does setup ask when I was last paid?

The last payday already happened, while the next one is a forecast. Each period runs from one payday up to, but not including, the next, so anchoring the schedule on a known date keeps later periods consistent.

Why is a listed bill not reserved from this paycheck?

If it is due on or after your next payday, it belongs to the next pay period. The home screen’s next-period preview shows bills reserved there.

The figure does not match my account

Open the current-period breakdown and choose Reconcile. Enter what you can actually use. The app records the difference as a visible adjustment rather than replacing your earlier entries.

Changing currency does not convert amounts

That is intentional. The app has no exchange-rate feed, so changing currency changes the label without changing the number.

Move to a new iPhone

Restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup. There is no separate account, sign-in, or export service.

Delete local data

Settings → Delete all data erases pay, bills, spending, and adjustments. Deleting the app also removes appearance and language preferences. SynoriTech has no server copy.

Purchases and refunds

Paycheck Budget is a paid download with no subscription or in-app purchase. Re-download it with the same Apple Account. Apple handles eligible refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contacting us

Include the app version, iOS version, steps, expected result, and actual result. Never send passwords, payment-card numbers, or bank details. We aim to reply within three business days.
