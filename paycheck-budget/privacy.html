Paycheck Budget

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Paycheck Budget has no account, bank connection, analytics, advertising, or server. Everything you enter stays in the app’s storage on your iPhone. SynoriTech never receives it.

Information stored on your device

  • Your pay amount, frequency, and payday schedule.
  • Bill names, amounts, due dates, and recurrence.
  • Spending entries, optional notes, and reconciliation adjustments.
  • Appearance and language preferences.

The app also stores a small home-summary snapshot in its own App Group container on the same device. It contains the current safe-to-spend amount, currency, coverage date, and setup state—never bill names, spending notes, or bank information.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, email address, password, or bank credentials.
  • It cannot connect to a bank or read an account balance.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDK.
  • It does not use an advertising identifier or track you across apps or websites.

Device backups

The database is stored in the iOS Application Support directory and can be included in a device backup. If you back up to iCloud or a computer, Apple may include it in that backup under Apple’s terms. The backup is controlled by you and does not go to SynoriTech.

Retention and deletion

Settings → Delete all data erases the pay schedule, bills, spending entries, adjustments, and home-summary snapshot. Appearance and language preferences remain until you delete the app. SynoriTech holds no copy and cannot recover deleted information.

External links and website hosting

The app accesses the internet only when you tap Support, Privacy, or Terms and iOS opens the corresponding page. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. SynoriTech does not add analytics or advertising to them.

Not financial advice

The app performs arithmetic on figures you enter. It is not financial, tax, legal, or investment advice and cannot verify that your entries match a real account.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. Questions can be sent to support@synoritech.com.
