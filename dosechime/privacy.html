# Privacy Policy Effective date: August 11, 2026 DoseChime is an offline-first medication reminder app. We do not require an account and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. ## Data stored on your device DoseChime stores the information you enter on your device, such as medication names, reminder schedules, dose logs, quiet hours, language preferences, theme preferences, and backup settings. This data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it. ## Backups If you use Backup & Restore, DoseChime creates a backup file on your device. You control where that file is saved or shared. We do not upload backups to our servers. ## Notifications DoseChime uses local notifications to remind you about scheduled doses. Notification delivery depends on your device settings, operating system, Focus / Do Not Disturb settings, battery settings, and connected wearable devices. ## Purchases If you unlock DoseChime, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. ## Analytics and tracking DoseChime does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. ## Website hosting This privacy policy may be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical information when you visit this page, according to GitHub’s own privacy practices. ## Deleting your data You can delete DoseChime data by deleting the app from your device. You can also delete any backup files you created. ## Contact If you have questions, contact: support@example.com
